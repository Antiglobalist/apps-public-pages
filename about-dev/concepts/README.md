## Design directions

All variants use the same verified scope: products from scratch; backend; Android, iOS, Telegram and Wear OS; App Store and Google Play listing preparation; analytics and in-app payment integration; 10+ years of experience; advanced practical AI development skills. English remains the public page language. Russian is used only for the local comparison gallery.

### Foundation

Existing design retained. Paper #f5f7fb, ink #182c48, blue #244cce, pale #e8edfa, white #ffffff. Space Grotesk headings and DM Sans body. Left-aligned split hero with an architecture illustration; capability columns and a distinct AI section.

### Product System

Plum #211b32, panel #302740, text #f7f2ff, muted #bcb2cb, lilac #dfc2ff. Outfit display and DM Sans body. Left-aligned vertical headline paired with a product ecosystem diagram. Broad service rows instead of cards. The diagram explains the backend/client relationship; it is not fabricated project work.

    headline | platform orbit
    full-width capability rows
    AI argument | practical skills

### Independent Studio

Sage paper #f1f5ee, forest #163f32, mint #c2e5d1, yellow #f2d786, muted #51695d. DM Serif Display headlines and DM Sans body. Broad editorial hero and a separate playful platform composition; a personal, single-developer voice. The palette and app-platform shapes replace generic cream/terracotta editorial styling.

    wide headline
    personal introduction | contact
    four platform shapes
    perspective | service descriptions

### Build to Launch

Paper #fff9f3, ink #292320, orange #f07842, peach #ffdbc4, muted #726057. Barlow Condensed display and Barlow body. Tall action headline with a stepped illustration, then a genuine three-stage delivery sequence. Numbering describes the process, not decorative section indices.

    Idea.  | ascending launch steps
    Build. | explanation
    Launch.| contact
    foundation > clients > launch

The concepts deliberately vary composition, typography, geometry, tone and section rhythm, not only colors. All use semantic headings, visible keyboard focus, responsive layouts and reduced-motion handling. No fabricated testimonials, metrics or portfolio projects.
